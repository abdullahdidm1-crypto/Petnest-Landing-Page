What Decides Pet Courier Service Cost in India?

Unlike ordinary parcel delivery, pet relocation requires living animal welfare protocols. Here are the 6 core factors that determine your quote.

📍

1. Distance and Route

Total kilometers directly influence toll, fuel, and handler transit hours for road journeys, as well as airline freight segments. Connecting flights or remote pincodes require multi-stage coordination.

✈️

2. Travel Mode

Air cargo is fastest for cross-country moves over 1,000 km. Indian Railways First AC coupe travel provides generous space at a lower cost. Dedicated road cabs offer custom doorstep convenience.

📦

3. Pet Size & Crate Dimensions

Airlines calculate charges using volumetric weight (L × W × H / 5000). A large Labrador requires an IATA-compliant size 500 crate, which occupies substantial cargo hold space.

🐕

4. Breed & Snout Anatomy

Short-nosed (brachycephalic) breeds like Pugs and Persian cats face airline cargo restrictions. These breeds typically travel via private First AC train coupes or specialized road cabs.

☀️

5. Season and Availability

During peak summer months, airlines enforce tarmac temperature limits, necessitating morning or night flight bookings. Festival seasons also tighten train coupe availability.

🩺

6. Veterinary Clearances

Mandatory health-cum-fitness certificates, rabies verification, local transit documentation, and terminal assistance represent important parts of compliant transit.

Pet Relocation & Transport Cost FAQs

Clear answers to common questions regarding pet transport service cost, airline and train rates.

The overall pet transport cost in India depends on distance, chosen mode (flight, train coupe, or road cab), pet weight, and crate dimensions. Every move receives a complete itemised breakdown before booking so you know exactly what is included.
Generally, Indian Railways First AC coupe travel offers a lower to moderate cost level for medium-to-long distances, especially for large dogs. Direct road transport is cost-effective for regional moves under 800 km. Air cargo carries higher freight charges but is the fastest option for moves exceeding 1,000 km.
If you already own an IATA-compliant crate matching your pet's size, you can use it and save on crate purchase costs. If you need a crate, we provide IATA-approved airline crates or guide you in selecting the right certified model.
