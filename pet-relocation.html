Choosing The Right Journey

Travel Modes Explained: Which Is Best When?

Compare transport options in India to choose the safest, lowest-stress travel mode for your pet.

🚐
Direct Road

Dedicated AC Pet Taxi

Private air-conditioned pet taxi with non-slip mats and pet first aid, accompanied by a trained handler.

Which is best when: Trips under 800 km, anxious pets, flat-faced breeds, or multi-pet families.
🚆
Railways

First AC Train Coupe

Reserved private First AC compartment allowing owners or our escort to stay with the pet throughout.

Which is best when: Interstate journeys where pet parents prefer continuous physical contact, or large dogs.
✈️
Air Transit

IATA Air Cargo

Pets fly in specialized temperature-regulated, pressurized aircraft holds under international live animal rules.

Which is best when: Long-distance moves (e.g. Delhi to Bengaluru) and overseas travel where minimal transit time is key.
💺
Accompanied

In-Cabin Flight

Pet travels under the forward seat inside an approved soft carrier on pet-friendly domestic carriers.

Which is best when: Small dogs and cats under 7 kg traveling on the exact same flight with their human parent.

Frequently Asked Questions

Key details about preparing your pet for domestic or international transit across India.

Professional pet transport services manage door-to-door transit between cities. We assess pet temperament, coordinate veterinary fitness certificates, supply IATA travel crates, and oversee road, train, or flight transit with live WhatsApp updates.

Dedicated AC road transport is safest for anxious pets under 800 km due to frequent potty and hydration stops. For long distances, First AC train coupes keep you with your pet, while flight cargo offers the fastest transit.

Pricing depends on distance, transit mode (private AC cab, train escort, or flight cargo), pet weight and crate size, and vet documentation. ThePetNest provides upfront, itemized quotes with zero hidden charges.

Domestic moves require valid Rabies and core vaccination records plus a Fit-to-Travel certificate from a registered vet within 24 to 72 hours of departure. Rules vary by airline, state, and destination country, and we guide you through the paperwork. Please verify with official authorities like AQCS.

Select airlines allow pets under 7 kg (including carrier) in the cabin with advance booking. Larger pets travel securely in pressurized, temperature-regulated cargo holds inside IATA crates.

IATA guidelines require sufficient space to stand erect, turn, and lie down naturally. We measure length and standing height to supply sturdy crates with 4-side ventilation and spill-safe bowls.

Pets receive a light meal 4 to 6 hours before departure to prevent nausea. Water is accessible throughout transit. On road trips, handlers stop every 3 to 4 hours in secure green areas for walks.
