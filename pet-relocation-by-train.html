Quick Answer for Pet Parents

Are pets allowed in train in India?

Yes, pets are allowed in Indian Railways trains, but only under specific, predefined railway regulations. Pets are not freely permitted in general passenger coaches. Depending on your travel class and budget, Indian Railways provides two authorized transportation pathways: travelling inside an exclusively booked First Class AC (1AC) coupe/cabin, or being booked as luggage inside the Guard Van Dog Box (Brake Van).

✓ Permitted with Owner

First Class AC (1AC) Coupe

Your dog or cat travels right beside you in comfort. You must book all berths in a 2-berth coupe or 4-berth cabin on a single PNR, obtain a veterinary certificate, and complete luggage weighment at the station parcel office.

⚠ Guard Van Only

Brake Van Dog Box (SLR)

If travelling in Sleeper or 2AC/3AC, pets cannot enter the coach. They must be booked at the railway parcel counter to travel in the special iron Dog Box in the guard van. The owner is responsible for feeding and water during station halts.

✓ In Secure Carrier

Cats & Small Animals

Cats, rabbits, and birds can travel in 1AC coupes inside leakproof, ventilated crates, or inside the luggage van. They must never roam loose inside any railway compartment or platform area.

Important Regulatory Notice: Carrying pets inside AC 2-Tier, AC 3-Tier, AC Chair Car, or Sleeper passenger coaches is strictly prohibited under Section 158 of the Railways Act. Passengers must verify current railway rules, train coach compositions, coupe allocation availability, and station parcel desk timings well before departure.

Rules for Travelling with Dogs

Dogs are permitted on Indian Railways trains under strict accommodation rules. For passenger coach travel, you must reserve the entire First AC coupe (2 berths) or cabin (4 berths).

  • Exclusive Compartment: If even one co-passenger in a shared compartment objects, the dog will be removed to the brake van dog box with no refund.
  • Owner Liability: Owners are solely responsible for pet conduct, cleanliness, hygiene, and any physical damage to railway property.
  • Leash & Muzzle: Dogs must remain leashed and muzzled while navigating platforms and train gangways.

Rules for Cats & Smaller Pets

Cats, rabbits, birds, and small companion animals can travel on trains subject to strict carrier guidelines and veterinary health clearance.

  • Carrier Requirement: Cats must always remain inside secure, hard-shell or heavy-duty fabric carriers with breathable mesh and leakproof base.
  • Passenger Coach Access: Permitted inside First AC coupe/cabins when booked exclusively.
  • Brake Van Transit: If not travelling in 1AC, cats must be booked as luggage in the brake van inside sturdy crates with food/water provisions.

Step-by-Step Instructions

How to Book a Train Ticket for a Dog Online

The honest reality: IRCTC does not sell standalone dog tickets online. Pet travel is a hybrid process combining online passenger ticketing with in-person station luggage booking. Here is the verified procedure.

1

Book First AC Passenger Tickets Online via IRCTC

IRCTC Online

Log into your IRCTC account. Choose a train equipped with a First Class AC (1A) coach. Book 2 passenger tickets with "Coupe" preference, or 4 tickets for "Cabin". Ensure tickets are confirmed under a single PNR.

2

Submit Written Coupe Allocation Request Letter

Offline Railway Office

24 to 48 hours before train departure, visit the DRM office or Station Director. Submit a formal representation requesting a coupe for pet transit with confirmed PNR copies and vaccination proofs.

3

Obtain Certified Veterinary Health Certificate

Veterinary Clinic

Visit a registered veterinarian within 24 to 48 hours of journey to obtain a Fit-to-Travel health certificate confirming your pet is clinically healthy and rabies immunizations are active.

4

Complete Pet Weighment & Luggage Booking at Station

Station Parcel Counter

Arrive 3 to 4 hours early at the railway parcel/luggage booking counter, pay applicable luggage scale charges, and collect the physical Pet Luggage Ticket before boarding your First AC coupe.
