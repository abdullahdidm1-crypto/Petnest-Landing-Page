Reliable Pet Transport Service by Air, Train & Road

Every pet has unique physical needs, size, and anxiety levels. We help you choose the best pet transport mode across India.

Fastest for Long Distances
Air Pet Transport Visual Temperature-regulated cargo & in-cabin

Pet Transport by Flight

Fast air travel via temperature-controlled airline cargo or in-cabin slots. Handlers manage airport security, crate clearance, and destination handover.

Best for: Moves >1,000 km, time-sensitive relocations, calm pets.
Stress Level: Moderate (shortest city-to-city transit time).
Suitability: Small pets in-cabin; medium/large dogs in cargo.
Note: IATA crates and vet certificates within 24-72 hours required. Short-nosed breeds restricted.
Maximum Comfort & Space
Train Pet Transport Visual Private First AC coupe & guard box

Pet Transport by Train

Comfortable rail travel inside a reserved First AC (1A) coupe cabin with owners, or via train parcel guard boxes. Handlers assist with station bookings.

Best for: Large dogs, flight-anxious pets, routes of 400-1,500 km.
Stress Level: Very Low (spacious private cabin, familiar company).
Suitability: Dogs and cats of all sizes and breeds.
Note: Coupe allotment depends on railway charts. Requires counter booking with vet records.
100% Direct Door-to-Door
Road Pet Transport Visual Dedicated AC vehicle with scheduled rest stops

Dedicated Road Pet Taxi

A private air-conditioned vehicle exclusively dedicated to your pet with a trained attendant. Direct door-to-door transit with rest stops every 3-4 hours.

Best for: Routes <1,000 km, multiple pets, senior dogs, flat-faced breeds.
Stress Level: Low (continuous physical attendant care, private cabin).
Suitability: All dog breeds, cats in carriers, puppies.
Note: Travel time depends on highway conditions. Pets travel in secured crates with comfort mats.

Travel Readiness

Is Your Pet Ready for Domestic Travel? Pre-Trip Checklist

Review these 8 practical checklist items before your travel date to ensure a smooth, low-stress journey.

✓

Vaccination Records

Ensure annual core vaccinations and Rabies vaccines are updated at least 21 days prior to interstate travel.

✓

Vet Fitness Certificate

Schedule a health checkup with your registered vet within 24 to 72 hours before departure for formal clearance.

✓

Crate Acclimatization

Acclimate your pet to their travel crate 7 to 14 days ahead so they stand, turn, and rest comfortably.

✓

ID Tag & Microchip

Attach a secure collar tag with pet and owner phone numbers. Microchipping is optional but advised.

✓

Feeding & Water Plan

Feed a light meal 3 to 4 hours before departure to avoid nausea. Attach spill-proof water dispensers inside the crate.

✓

Familiar Scent Item

Place an unwashed blanket or t-shirt with home scent inside the crate to provide comforting reassurance.

✓

Breed Considerations

Consult your vet thoroughly for short-nosed breeds; climate-controlled ground transit is often preferred.

✓

Weather Planning

Schedule travel during cooler morning or evening hours in hot summer months to avoid heat stress.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Domestic Pet Transport in India

Clear answers to common questions regarding travel modes, veterinary documentation, safety, and quotes.

What is the difference between pet transport by flight, train, and road in India? +
Flight is quickest for distances over 1,000 km in temperature-regulated cargo or in-cabin slots. Train travel via Indian Railways provides private First AC coupe space for larger dogs. Dedicated road pet transport service in India offers door-to-door convenience with scheduled rest stops under 1,000 km.
Can short-nosed (brachycephalic) dog and cat breeds travel on domestic routes? +
Short-nosed breeds (Pugs, French Bulldogs, Persian cats) have sensitive airways and face airline cargo bans. For their safety, dedicated climate-controlled road transit or private First AC train coupes are recommended after veterinary evaluation.
Can large and giant breed dogs travel comfortably across Indian states? +
Yes. Large dogs travel in IATA-approved cargo crates on flights, reserved First AC train coupes, or dedicated road SUV cabs with ample room to stand, turn, and rest naturally.
How are cats safely relocated domestically without excessive stress? +
During pet relocation within India, cats travel in secure carriers lined with absorbent, home-scented bedding and breathable shade covers to minimize sensory stress, with spill-proof water dispensers.
How far in advance should I book domestic pet transport services? +
We suggest booking 2 to 3 weeks ahead to complete veterinary fitness checks, vaccination updates, crate training, and train coupe or airline cargo space bookings.
What veterinary documents and health certificates are required for domestic pet travel? +
Interstate travel requires an updated vaccination card (Rabies and core vaccines) and a Veterinary Fitness Certificate issued by a registered vet within 24 to 72 hours before departure. Guidelines vary by carrier and state; verify rules with authorities.
How do I track my pet during transit and receive updates? +
We provide live WhatsApp updates throughout transit. For road trips, handlers share location pins, photos, and video check-ins at rest stops. For flights and trains, you receive check-in and handover confirmations.
What factors determine the quote for domestic pet transport service? +
Quotes for pet transport service depend on travel mode (air, train, or road), route distance, pet weight, crate dimensions, and optional doorstep pickup. All quotes are transparent and itemized.
Is pet transport service near me available with doorstep pickup in Delhi NCR? +
Yes, we provide doorstep pet collection across Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, and Faridabad, transferring your pet directly to terminals or road routes.
What happens if a flight is delayed or a train is rescheduled during pet transit? +
Our team monitors schedules 24/7. If delays occur, your pet remains with our attendant in a climate-regulated holding area with fresh water and care until departure.
