Our Origin Story

Founded With Love for Pets Who Hate Car Rides & Metal Cages

ThePetNest began with a simple observation: pets get terrified when dragged into cramped car carriers, stuck in noisy traffic, and locked into cold salon cages waiting for their turn. Anxious dogs tremble, while sensitive cats get traumatized for days.

We believed pet parents and their furry family members deserved a kinder, cleaner, and more transparent alternative. So we created a service where certified pet stylists bring a fully sanitized salon kit directly into your home. You stay right beside your pet, witnessing every step with complete peace of mind.

As families began relocating across India, we expanded our expertise into safe, verified pet relocation—handling IRCTC train travel approvals, air cargo clearances, and dedicated AC pet taxis with 100% live GPS and WhatsApp check-ins.
